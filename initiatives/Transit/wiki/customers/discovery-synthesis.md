# Discovery synthesis — One Bus Ride (2026-09)

Condensed pointer to `One bus ride/02b_customer_discovery.md` (full 10-conversation synthesis). Kept here as the living reference for future Transit ideas, per `IDEA_LIFECYCLE.md`'s customer discovery wiki-update guidance.

**Result:** 8 of 10 confirmed the core problem; 8 of 10 said they'd use or share the result. Decision framework outcome: **Build.**

**Segments:** new transplants (sharpest pain), hosts (recurring dateable trigger), existing riders (familiar with the route, still lack a reason to explore), and a smaller skeptic segment (already have a car, question the "one bus ride" frame).

**Durable messaging insight:** "one bus ride" tests well as a hook; "your personal transit guide" tests poorly (too close to general trip planners). Avoid tourism-guide language.

**Open items carried to Growth:** whether MetroRapid 801 specifically is the best pilot corridor (2 of 10 questioned this) and whether the no-live-data seam holds up in sustained real use.
