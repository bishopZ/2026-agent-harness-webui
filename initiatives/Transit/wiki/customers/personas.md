# Personas — One Bus Ride

Drawn directly from customer discovery (`One bus ride/02b_customer_discovery.md`). Reusable reference for future Transit ideas targeting the same audience.

- **Dana (transplant)** — 29, moved to Austin 3 months ago, no car yet, already rides the 801 to commute but has never gotten off anywhere she didn't already have a destination.
- **Marcus (host)** — 34, hosts out-of-town friends often, currently defaults to driving them around because planning a car-free afternoon "feels like extra work."
- **Priya (existing rider)** — 21, UT student, rides the 801 daily, has "always meant to get off somewhere new" but never had a reason to.

**Secondary segment, not yet a named persona:** skeptics who already have a car and question whether "one bus ride" is a distinct category (discovery #6, #10) — useful as a negative-targeting signal, not a persona to design for.

## Journey (new-transplant path)

Moves to Austin → settles near a frequent corridor before owning a car → commutes but doesn't explore → has an open afternoon → currently defaults to either over-researching or staying in (`market/landscape.md`) → with One Bus Ride: picks a direction/budget → gets a bounded plan → goes.
