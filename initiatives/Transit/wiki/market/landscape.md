# Landscape — car-free outing planning (Austin)

Condensed from `One bus ride/02_market_research.md`. Reusable reference for future Transit ideas.

**General trip planners** (Google Maps, Citymapper, CapMetro's own app, Transit app) — `DATA`, comprehensive and live-data-backed, but assume the user already knows their destination.

**Local "things to do" content** — `DATA` that this category exists broadly; organized by neighborhood/category, not by "reachable by one specific ride, right now."

**Gap / wedge:** nothing occupies "a single bounded outing, reachable by one ride, printable, zero setup." A static, single-page result is also inherently link-shareable in a way a trip-planner session isn't.

**Analogous model:** bounded "one perfect day" travel-content itineraries reliably find an audience by removing decisions (`INFERENCE`) — same mechanism this initiative is betting on at neighborhood scale, different moment (unplanned, right now vs. planned ahead).

**Durable risk:** content tied to specific businesses ages; favor durable public landmarks/districts in any future Transit idea's data model, per `operations/build-stack-and-hosting.md`.
