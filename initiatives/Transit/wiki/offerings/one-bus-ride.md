# Offering — One Bus Ride

**What it is:** A free, static, single-page web app. Pick a direction, a boarding stop, and a time budget on MetroRapid 801; get a printable, shareable plan (board stop, alight stop, nearby things to do, a return-trip note, and a clear "not live data, verify before you go" disclaimer).

**Differentiator:** the constraint is the product — one route, no live data, no account — which is what makes the result fast to trust and fast to use for a low-stakes, unplanned outing. See `wiki/market/landscape.md` for how this compares to general trip planners and neighborhood guides.

**Status:** Live (`Growth` lifecycle stage as of 2026-09-12). Source: `Transit/One bus ride/`. Live artifact: `Transit/One bus ride/outputs/app/`.
