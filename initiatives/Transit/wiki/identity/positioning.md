# Transit — identity & positioning

## Mission

Make car-free exploration of Austin easier, one bounded idea at a time. Transit ideas favor small, shippable answers to specific "I have a constraint, what can I actually do" questions over general-purpose trip-planning tools that already exist and are already better resourced.

## Voice

Plain and practical. No tourism-guide hype ("hidden gems," "must-see") — customer discovery for One Bus Ride specifically flagged that language as sounding too close to the general guides this initiative differentiates against. Say what something is and where it is; let the reader decide if it's for them.

## Positioning statement — One Bus Ride

For car-free or car-light people in Austin who have an afternoon and one bus line, One Bus Ride is a free, no-account planning tool that turns exactly one MetroRapid 801 ride into a complete, printable outing — unlike general trip planners, which assume you already know your destination, and unlike neighborhood guides, which ignore whether anything is reachable without a car.

See [`offerings/one-bus-ride.md`](../offerings/one-bus-ride.md) for the deliverable itself, and `01_brief.md` / `03_prd.md` under `One bus ride/` for full detail.
