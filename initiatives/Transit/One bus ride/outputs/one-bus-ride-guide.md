# One bus ride — MetroRapid 801, Southbound, medium budget

A single CapMetro ride with a short walk-and-see stop at the end. Public places only. Not affiliated with Capital Metro — check [capmetro.org](https://www.capmetro.org/) for today's 801 times before you go.

## The ride

Board MetroRapid **801** at **North Lamar & Justin Ln (Crestview)**. Ride south through downtown toward South Congress.

## Alight and explore: Congress Ave & 11th (Capitol)

- **Texas State Capitol grounds** (public landmark, ~5 min walk) — Public state capitol building and grounds, open for walking and self-guided viewing.
- **Congress Avenue Bridge** (public landmark, ~10 min walk) — Public bridge over Lady Bird Lake, known for its evening bat colony in warmer months.

## Getting back

Catch the 801 back the way you came, or continue south to keep exploring (South 1st & Bouldin and South Congress are both further down the same line).

## Try it yourself

Open [`app/index.html`](app/index.html) to generate a plan for the other direction, a different boarding stop, or a shorter or longer time budget — and to print or share the result. Corridor data last verified 2026-09-11 (`app/stops.json`).
